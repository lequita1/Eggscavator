\# Eggscavator — Official Revamp Task Board



\## Purpose



This document defines the confirmed scope and direction of the Eggscavator revamp.



The entire game is NOT being rebuilt.



The existing excavation/gameplay foundation is considered good and should be preserved unless a specific part is later identified as needing improvement.



The revamp focuses on turning Eggscavator into a \*\*story-driven excavation adventure with simulator progression\*\*.



\---



\# CORE GAME VISION



Eggscavator should initially feel like a normal simulator:



```text

Find Egg

→ Excavate

→ Collect Materials

→ Sell

→ Earn Money

→ Upgrade Tools

→ Excavate Deeper

```



As the player progresses, the meaning of the loop gradually changes:



```text

Excavate

→ Discover Something Strange

→ Sell / Investigate It

→ Someone Takes Interest

→ Continue Excavating

→ Discover Signs of Life

→ Reach Core

→ Hatchling Reveal

→ Deliver Hatchling

→ Witness Reunion

→ Discover New Clue

→ Discover Next Egg

```



The long-term mystery is:



> Why do these extraordinary eggs exist, and how are they connected?



The first three chapters should NOT fully answer that question.



\---



\# CORE DESIGN PRINCIPLE



The player should discover the story while playing the game.



Story should not constantly interrupt the gameplay.



The excavation remains the primary gameplay.



The world, materials, NPCs, selling system, cutscenes, journal, and hatchling sequences reveal the story around that gameplay.



Target feeling:



```text

Fun

↓

Curiosity

↓

Suspicion

↓

Mystery

↓

Discovery

↓

Shock

↓

Emotional Payoff

↓

Bigger Mystery

```



\---



\# PRESERVE THE EXISTING EXCAVATION SYSTEM



The current patch-based excavation mechanic is a core foundation.



The egg remains a generally smooth egg-shaped object.



The player:



```text

Aim at Patch

→ Patch Highlights

→ Break Patch

→ Patch Disappears

→ Next Layer Revealed

→ Repeat

→ Reach Core

```



This mechanic should NOT be replaced with an unrelated mining system.



The revamp should improve:



\* visual quality

\* layer identity

\* material detail

\* crack feedback

\* particles

\* sound

\* impact feedback

\* atmosphere

\* camera presentation

\* progression storytelling



The player should still feel that they are physically excavating the same smooth egg one patch at a time.



\---



\# EGG VISUAL REVAMP



The smooth egg silhouette is preserved.



However, the patches should no longer feel like generic brown sections.



The layers should look like intentional materials belonging to that specific egg.



Each egg should have its own visual language.



Potential changes per layer:



\* material

\* color

\* roughness

\* surface texture

\* cracks

\* internal structure

\* particles

\* light

\* sound

\* environmental reaction



The deeper the player excavates, the more the material progression should communicate the nature of the egg.



The final layer/core must feel visually important.

\# INITIAL CHAPTERS



The first major revamp contains exactly three chapters:



```text

CHAPTER 1 — CHICKEN

CHAPTER 2 — DINOSAUR

CHAPTER 3 — ALIEN

```



Do not expand into additional egg types until these three chapters are properly designed and polished.



\---



\# CHAPTER 1 — CHICKEN EGG



\## Narrative Role



The Chicken Egg establishes the normal world.



The player should initially believe:



> "This is just an egg excavation simulator."



The chapter should be friendly, simple, and approachable.



\## Egg Design



The egg remains smooth and patch-based.



Possible progression:



```text

Normal Eggshell

→ Thick Shell

→ Calcium-Rich Layer

→ Inner Membrane

→ Warm Organic Layer

→ Yolk/Core

```



These layers should feel recognizable as an exaggerated giant chicken egg.



Avoid supernatural effects during the early portion of the chapter.



\## Selling



The player sells ordinary eggshell material to a farmer.



The farmer has a believable reason to buy it, such as agricultural or soil-related uses.



The early economy should feel completely normal.



\## Ending



The egg finally breaks.



A chick is revealed.



The player delivers the chick to its mother.



The mother recognizes the hatchling.



The reunion is cute and wholesome.



After the reunion, the mother produces another unusually large egg.



This is the player's first subtle clue that something about the eggs may not be normal.



\---



\# CHAPTER 2 — DINOSAUR EGG



\## Narrative Role



The Dinosaur Egg is the first major shift from ordinary simulator to mystery.



The player should start thinking:



> "Something is wrong with these eggs."



\## Egg Design



The egg remains smooth and patch-based.



Possible progression:



```text

Hardened Fossil Shell

→ Mineralized Shell

→ Fossil Layer

→ Amber Deposits

→ Ancient Bone Material

→ Unknown Organic Layer

→ Living Core

```



The exact number and implementation of layers should be determined after auditing the existing egg system.



\## Discovery Materials



Potential materials:



\* Fossil Fragment

\* Amber Fragment

\* Ancient Bone Fragment

\* Strange Mineral

\* Unknown Organic Material

\* Living Tissue Fragment



Avoid adding large quantities of meaningless materials.



Every major material should have either gameplay, economy, journal, or story purpose.



\---



\# A.R.C.



Introduce an original mysterious organization:



\*\*A.R.C. — Anomaly Research \& Containment\*\*



The organization should have its own identity and should not simply be called "Men in Black."



They may use:



\* black suits

\* black vehicles

\* earpieces

\* specialized equipment

\* professional containment procedures



The purpose is to investigate and contain unusual discoveries.



\---



\# FIRST A.R.C. ENCOUNTER



A.R.C. should appear after a significant unusual discovery.



The encounter should be short.



Example tone:



```text

"Where did you find this?"



"Inside the egg."



"How deep?"



"Pretty deep."



"Who else knows?"

```



The purpose is to create questions.



Do not explain the entire world through dialogue.



\---



\# DR. VALE



The player can contact a knowledgeable researcher/advisor.



Working name:



\*\*Dr. Vale\*\*



Dr. Vale recognizes that the material is highly unusual and tells the player that:



\* it is valuable

\* it is not ordinary excavation material

\* there may be more inside

\* the player should investigate carefully



Dr. Vale should provide direction without revealing the complete mystery.



\---



\# A.R.C. PLAYER CHOICE



The player eventually chooses whether to cooperate with A.R.C.



\## COOPERATE



The player answers basic questions.



The player does not have to reveal everything.



Possible effects:



\* improved A.R.C. relationship

\* different dialogue

\* additional information

\* special opportunities

\* potential rewards

\* future warnings/access



\## REFUSE



The player refuses.



A.R.C. warns the player about the material.



The player continues their excavation/selling activity.



Possible effects:



\* different dialogue

\* reduced A.R.C. trust

\* independent discoveries

\* alternative opportunities



\## CHOICE RULE



The choice must not simply mean:



```text

YES = GOOD

NO = BAD

```



Both paths remain viable.



The choice primarily influences relationships, information, dialogue, and opportunities.



\---



\# DINOSAUR DISCOVERY ESCALATION



As the player excavates:



```text

Normal Fossil Material

→ Unusual Material

→ Organic Structure

→ Signs of Life

→ Heartbeat

```



The player eventually realizes:



> Something is alive inside this egg.



The realization should emerge naturally from the excavation.



\---



\# DINOSAUR ENDING



The final layer breaks.



The core opens.



A dinosaur hatchling is revealed.



The player interacts with and delivers the hatchling.



The reunion reveals a prehistoric parent.



A mysterious portal/rift may explain the parent's appearance.

```text

Mysterious
```

Do not reveal the entire explanation.

\# CHAPTER LOADING SCREEN



The game should not immediately drop players into a fully visible chapter scene if that causes noticeable asset pop-in or lag.



Upon arrival:



```text

→ Chapter Loading Interface

→ Load Critical Assets

→ Prepare Character / Camera / Chapter

→ Finish Initialization

→ Fade Into Intro

```



The loading sequence should focus on critical assets rather than blindly preloading the entire Workspace.



Do not create a fake progress indicator that claims an exact percentage when the loading system cannot provide one accurately.

\# ASSET CREATION PIPELINE — IMPORTANT



Claude must NOT attempt to directly generate or invent final 3D models as the primary asset-production method.



Claude's responsibility is to determine what asset is required, specify it accurately, and choose the best production path.



\## PRIMARY METHOD — BLENDER PYTHON



When a custom 3D asset can be created procedurally or accurately through Blender scripting, Claude should create a \*\*Blender Python script\*\*.



Claude may provide:



\* procedural modeling scripts

\* geometry generation

\* modifiers

\* materials

\* UV setup

\* scene setup

\* rig preparation

\* naming

\* export preparation



The user can run the script in Blender and review the resulting model.



This is the preferred method when procedural generation can achieve the required quality and accuracy.



\---



\# ALTERNATIVE METHOD — GEMINI → MESHY



When procedural Blender modeling is not the best way to achieve the desired visual quality, use this pipeline:



```text

Claude

→ Detailed concept specification

→ Google Gemini image-generation prompt

→ User generates reference/concept image in Gemini

→ User sends image to Meshy.ai

→ Meshy Image-to-3D

→ User reviews/refines model

→ Import into Roblox

```



Claude should provide the \*\*Gemini prompt\*\*, not pretend to directly create the final concept model.



The Gemini prompt should specify:



\* subject

\* silhouette

\* anatomy

\* proportions

\* pose

\* camera angle

\* visual style

\* material appearance

\* color direction

\* important details

\* Roblox suitability

\* background requirements when relevant



The resulting Gemini image is a \*\*reference/concept asset\*\*, which can then be supplied to Meshy.



\---



\# ASSET DECISION PROCESS



For every new custom asset, Claude should first determine:



```text

1\. What asset is required?

2\. Why is it required?

3\. Does an existing project asset already solve the problem?

4\. Can Roblox-native construction achieve the required quality?

5\. Can Blender Python achieve better accuracy/control?

6\. Would Gemini → Meshy produce a better result?

7\. Does the asset require rigging?

8\. Does it require animation?

9\. What dimensions/proportions are required?

10\. What performance constraints apply?

11\. What output format is required?

12\. Where will it be used?

```



Then Claude should recommend \*\*one primary method\*\* rather than making the user guess.

\# IMPLEMENTATION PROCESS



Before making a major change:



```text

1\. Inspect the existing implementation.

2\. Identify reusable systems.

3\. Identify systems that need extension.

4\. Identify systems that require refactoring.

5\. Identify systems that actually need replacement.

6\. Determine dependencies.

7\. Create an implementation plan.

8\. Identify required external assets.

9\. Request those assets using the defined Blender/Gemini/Meshy pipeline.

10\. Implement in logical stages.

11\. Test each stage.

12\. Update project documentation.

13\. Commit meaningful changes.

```



Do not perform a blind rewrite.

\# SUCCESS CRITERIA



The revamp is successful when:



\### Gameplay



The original patch-based egg excavation remains satisfying.



\### Chapters



Chicken, Dinosaur, and Alien feel like distinct chapters of one game.



\### Story



The story is discovered naturally through gameplay.



\### Selling



Selling remains useful for earning money while also becoming a narrative trigger.



\### Loading



Players do not experience unnecessary visual chaos or asset pop-in when entering a chapter.



\### Tutorial



Each chapter teaches the core game loop naturally through its introductory cutscene.



\### Hatchlings



Each hatchling has a memorable reveal and reunion.



\### Choices



The A.R.C. choice creates meaningful differences without unnecessary branch explosion.



\### Assets



Custom assets are produced through a deliberate, quality-focused pipeline using \*\*Blender Python as the primary method\*\* and \*\*Gemini → Meshy as the alternative when appropriate\*\*.



\### Multiplayer



Up to four players can experience the chapter together.



\### Mystery



Each chapter answers some questions while creating larger ones.



\### Existing Game



Working systems are preserved wherever possible.
